# Symmetry target for the square periodic ground state

The new study uses Lx=Ly=L, periodic boundaries in both directions, isotropic
nearest-neighbor hopping t=1, onsite interaction U/2*n*(n-1), U=16.74, and a
fixed particle number N0=L². The same occupation cutoff applies to every site.
This identifies the intended microscopic spatial/internal symmetries before
adding a group-equivariant NQS. No1D or Julia integration change is involved.

## Internal symmetry versus spatial symmetry

The single-component Bose–Hubbard model conserves total particle number. Its
internal unitary symmetry is U(1), generated by Nhat=sum_i n_i. A fixed-N state
transforms under exp(i*phi*Nhat) with the one-dimensional character exp(i*N*phi).
N0=9 is a charge-nine representation, not the charge-zero/trivial U(1) irrep.
Its ray is unchanged by this overall phase.

This is different from internal SU(2) rotations of an isotropic spin model.
Bosons can carry internal spin, but the current model has a single component.
Even the hard-core-boson/spin-half mapping normally produces an XY model,
which has U(1) rather than full isotropic SU(2) symmetry.

Bose particle-exchange statistics are already encoded in the occupation basis.
They do not make lattice rotations/reflections commute. A quarter-turn and a
mirror are noncommuting spatial operations, independent of particle statistics.

## Spatial group and irreps

The geometric space group of the square torus is

    G_space = (Z_L × Z_L) semidirect D4,
    G_unitary = U(1)_N × G_space.

The two translations commute, but rotations/reflections act on their directions,
so the space group is not a direct product of translation and point groups.
D4 is also called C4v in the planar square convention and has eight elements.
This is the geometric symmetry used for the ansatz; accidental extra symmetries
of particular tiny graphs are not presumed for generalL. For L=2 some nominal
group operations induce the same site permutation, so that action is not faithful.

Translation irreps are labeled by k=(2*pi/L)*(mx,my), with mx,my=0,...,L-1.
At k=0 the point group is the full D4, whose character table is:

| Irrep | Dimension | Identity | ±90° rotations | 180° | Axis mirrors | Diagonal mirrors |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A1 |1|1|1|1|1|1|
| A2 |1|1|1|1|−1|−1|
| B1 |1|1|−1|1|1|−1|
| B2 |1|1|−1|1|−1|1|
| E |2|2|0|−2|0|0|

Each column entry is the character of one element in that conjugacy class;
the identity's character gives the irrep dimension. For E the operations are
two-by-two matrices, not scalar parity signs. Typical patterns are s-like A1,
d_(x²−y²)-like B1, d_xy-like B2, and an (x,y) doublet E. The table appears, for
example, in [Chen et al., NeurIPS2022, Table1](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/file/3173c427cb4ed2d5eaab029c17f221ae-Paper-Conference.pdf).

At nonzero k, use irreps of the little group that preserves k modulo reciprocal
lattice vectors, together with its momentum star. In general a full space-group
irrep is not an arbitrary independent pair of momentum and a D4 label.
Spinless time reversal is also present: complex conjugation in the occupation
basis, with T²=+1. It is antiunitary and is listed separately from G_unitary.
It commutes with the real spatial permutations and conjugates number rotations:
`K V(phi) K^-1 = V(-phi)`. Products of these operators generate the corresponding
antiunitary symmetries as well; K is not an additional unitary spatial element.

## Ground-state sector

For finite t>0 on this connected, unfrustrated lattice, the fixed-N Hamiltonian
has nonpositive off-diagonal hopping matrix elements and an irreducible
occupation-configuration graph. Perron–Frobenius gives a unique strictly positive
ground vector within that sector. A spatial symmetry only permutes occupations;
the transformed vector must be the same positive ground vector. Therefore

    Target: (U1 charge N0, momentum (0,0), point-group A1).

This is spatial invariance, not an SU(2) spin singlet. It applies to the finite
canonical state even near criticality. It does not assert that N0 is the
grand-canonical ground sector at mu=0 or establish infinite-system symmetry
breaking. Other energy eigenstates can occupy nontrivial spatial irreps.

The existing independent3×3 ED state is audited in
`benchmarks/results/2026-11-02-square-symmetry/ed-symmetry-audit.json`:
all72 geometric space-group permutations, generator Hamiltonian invariance,
translation projectors and all five D4 character projectors. This audit reuses
the saved ED vector and performs no training, MC sampling or new eigensolve.

## Consequence for the next NQS

The current user-authorized study now includes symmetry verification, an explicit
regular-representation group CNN, Adam + normalized log-MSE fitting on the 3×3
torus, and a separate detailed teaching presentation. No VMC is part of this study.

Use spatially equivariant hidden features and a k=0/A1 invariant scalar
log-amplitude readout. Hidden features may contain every D4 irrep, including E,
even though the final output is A1. A regular-representation G-CNN can implement
these features as orientation/reflection channels; no internal SU(2) machinery
is required. Spatial invariance does not impose zero off-sector amplitude:
fixed-number support is still handled separately.

First qualify on the3×3 square torus with Adam + normalized log-MSE and raw ED
overlap/energy checks. The working3×3 gate is absolute unsquared overlap≥0.99;
report energy/variance separately. Verify network invariance before and after
fitting. No VMC extension or larger-system campaign precedes this qualification.

Primary architecture references: [Cohen & Welling2016](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v48/cohenc16.html),
[Roth & MacDonald2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.05085).

### Implemented group CNN

`models.GroupEquivariantConv2DNet` uses one lifting layer, one true group
convolution, SiLU after each, mean pooling over sites and orientations, and a
linear scalar head. The constructor defaults to 16 feature channels; this gives
19,201 trainable parameters at cutoff 4. It accepts square fully periodic
geometries only. All six older architecture definitions and their checkpoint
behavior remain unchanged; the default benchmark sweep still contains five.

Use `R(x,y)=(-y,x)` and `M(x,y)=(x,-y)` about the origin, wrapping sites modulo L.
Point-group index `r+4*m` denotes `R**r M**m`, with the right operation applied
first. For `g=(t,q)`, the input action and regular-feature action are

    (g x)(r) = x(q^-1(r-t)),
    (g F)(r,h) = F(q^-1(r-t),q^-1 h).

One-hot channel c is `x_c(r)=1` exactly when `n(r)=c`. With centered stencil
offsets delta in {-1,0,1}² and h,a in D4, the implementation computes

    F1_o(r,h) = SiLU[b_o + sum_(delta,c) K_oc(h^-1 delta) x_c(r+delta)],
    F2_o(r,h) = SiLU[b_o + sum_(delta,a,c)
                         W_oc(h^-1 delta,h^-1 a) F1_c(r+delta,a)],
    z_c = mean_(r,h) F2_c(r,h),
    f_theta(n) = linear(z).

This is cross-correlation, matching PyTorch's input at r+delta convention.
The relative orientation `h^-1 a` ties the weights while mixing all eight
input orientations. Biases are shared across sites and orientations. Because
the group only permutes these regular-feature coordinates, pointwise SiLU is
equivariant. Pooling removes that permutation to give an invariant scalar.
Using an arbitrary componentwise activation on signed two-dimensional E-irrep
coordinates would require a different nonlinear construction.

`lift_features`, `group_features`, and `forward_features` expose
`(batch,8,hidden_dim,L,L)` fields for independent layer checks. `forward` keeps
the existing real `(batch,)` unnormalized-log-amplitude contract. In particular,
it does not impose a total-N mask. The physical ket in this study is defined
only in the fixed-N sector; an all-sector use would need an explicit projector.

The predeclared supervised demonstration uses width 16, Adam, learning rate
0.001, seed 123, exactly 10,000 updates, GPU1 float64 and TF32 disabled. It selects
strictly by normalized log-MSE, with no early stop, fidelity/energy selection,
infidelity training or architecture grid. `benchmarks/train_square_group.py`
implements the exact orbit-weighted objective and directly reevaluates the
saved selected checkpoint on all 19,855 configurations in CPU float64. Within-
orbit target means and variances preserve even the tiny ED roundoff deviations
from exact invariance. Snapshots every 100 updates are descriptive; checkpoint
selection checks the loss after every update.

## What remains after imposing symmetry?

An irrep dimension is different from its multiplicity in the Hilbert space.
There are many independent copies of A1. More generally,

    H_N = direct_sum_lambda (V_lambda tensor M_lambda),
    H = direct_sum_lambda (identity_on_V_lambda tensor h_lambda).

Commuting with the group constrains the irrep factor V_lambda but permits a
nontrivial Hamiltonian h_lambda on the multiplicity space M_lambda. Specifying
N,k=0,A1 selects a block, not its lowest eigenvector.

For the3×3 torus, N=9/cutoff4, the19,855 configurations fall into338 orbits
under the72 geometric space-group operations. This agrees independently with
Burnside's count: the sum of fixed-configuration counts is24,336, so the number
of orbits is24,336/72=338. Orbit sizes range from1 to72; simply dividing the
total basis dimension by72 would be incorrect.

For each orbit O_a, use the normalized basis vector

    |O_a> = (1/sqrt(|O_a|)) sum_(n in O_a) |n>,
    |psi> = sum_a c_a |O_a>,
    psi(n in O_a) = c_a/sqrt(|O_a|).

The positive real normalized ground state therefore still needs338 positive
coefficients subject to one normalization condition (337 continuous parameters).
Our sparse338×338 reduced Hamiltonian has5455 nonzero entries and reproduces
the existing full-ED ground vector with reconstruction residual1.35e-15 and
reduced eigenvector residual1.36e-14. This remains an exact dynamical eigenproblem.

One hole–doublon pair on a nearest-neighbor bond and one on a diagonal belong
to different orbits, each with36 configurations. They have the same occupation
histogram and onsite interaction energy, but geometry prevents a space-group
operation from mapping one into the other. At U/t=16.74 their ED amplitudes per
configuration are0.0887651 and0.0477907, respectively: ratio1.85737. Symmetry
does not fix this ratio; hopping and interactions do.

Likewise, translation fixes uniform rho_i=N/Nsites and quarter-turn symmetry
fixes Gx=Gy, but neither fixes the coherence magnitude, onsite fluctuations,
pair-separation dependence or higher-order correlations. These remain dynamical
information that the NQS must approximate as an invariant function.

The reduction is substantial for this tiny cluster but is not a general solution
of the many-body problem: the geometric group has only8L² elements, while the
fixed-density Hilbert space still grows exponentially with L². When fitting
only orbit representatives, use orbit multiplicities in full-sector normalization
and log-MSE weights; equal representative weights would change the objective.
