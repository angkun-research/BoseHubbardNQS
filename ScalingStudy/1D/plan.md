# 1D Bose–Hubbard scaling study

The [pilot results and next experiments](README.md) record what was
completed under this study design.

Measure the neural quantum state (NQS) capacity, training compute, and sampling needed to reach fixed physical accuracy in the superfluid (SF), at the Berezinskii–Kosterlitz–Thouless (BKT) point, and in the deep Mott insulator (MI). Compare with the bond dimensions that ring DMRG actually needs at the same tolerances. Numerical budgets and acceptance rules below are proposed and will be locked at the indicated stage boundaries.

## Model and expectations

Use periodic rings at fixed $N=L$ with $t=1$ and zero chemical potential. The onsite coefficient of $n_i(n_i-1)/2$ is $U_{\rm phys}$. The three $U_{\rm phys}/t$ values are 2, 3.275, and 8, corresponding to legacy `U_code` values 1, 1.6375, and 4. Use inclusive `Nmax=6`, with matched SF reference runs at `Nmax=8` at $L=16,32$ and the largest size if cutoff shifts approach the reference margin. Report shifts in energy per site and required correlations and resolve any material sensitivity before pooling sizes. The published critical estimate is $3.275(2)$ and the earlier local reproduction gave $3.27602$ with analysis sensitivity $3.27399$–$3.27617$. Record these separately from numerical reference errors at the exact simulated coupling. [CriticalBehavior results](../../CriticalBehavior/RESULTS.md) · [Thamm et al.](https://doi.org/10.1103/4xdz-5vq7)

Define $G_1(r)=L^{-1}\sum_i\langle b_i^\dagger b_{i+r}\rangle$ with periodic indices. The gapless SF has $c=1$, $K>2$ in this convention, and $G_1(r)\sim r^{-1/(2K)}$. At BKT, $c=1$, $K_c=2$, and $z=1$. The leading critical form is $G_1(r)\propto r^{-1/4}[\log(r/r_0)]^{1/8}$ at long distances, and $K(L)$ approaches 2 logarithmically. The gapped MI has an exponentially decaying $G_1$, saturating block-number variance, and finite one-body correlation length $\xi$ at fixed coupling, with $\log\xi\sim A/\sqrt{U_{\rm phys}/t-U_c/t}$ on approaching BKT from the Mott side. [CriticalBehavior results](../../CriticalBehavior/RESULTS.md) · [Thamm et al.](https://doi.org/10.1103/4xdz-5vq7) · [Kiely and Mueller](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.105.134502) · [Rams et al.](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.8.041033)

For a fixed fraction of the ring, the bipartite entanglement grows logarithmically with $L$ in the two gapless cases and saturates in the gapped MI. Uniform MPS finite-entanglement theory gives $\xi_\chi\sim\chi^{1.344}$ for $c=1$. Matching $\xi_\chi$ to $L$ motivates a crossover $\chi\sim L^{0.744}$ in both gapless cases. Treat this as a guide for the bond sweep and estimate the finite-ring, tolerance-dependent slopes independently. At fixed gapped coupling, test for a plateau in the bond dimension required by these observable tolerances once $L\gg\xi$. Global fidelity can remain size dependent as local errors accumulate. [Pollmann et al.](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.102.255701) · [Kiely and Mueller](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.105.134502) · [Pirvu et al.](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.86.075117)

An optional Mott crossover point is $U_{\rm phys}/t=1/0.21\approx4.76$. Table III gives $\xi_{G_1}=16.69(10)$ at the same cutoff. Independently estimate $\xi$ from DMRG $G_1$ using converged long rings with periodic image contributions and fit-window sensitivity. Allow longer reference-only rings if needed. Compare the two Mott couplings against $L/\xi$ and carry the fit uncertainty into the comparison. Treat a common curve as an empirical question. [Rams et al., Table III](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1801.08554)

## Exact screen and qualification

The eight defined classes include the registered `FCNet`, `RBMNet`, `Conv1DNet`, and `JastrowNet`, plus the experimental `Conv1DPoolNet`, `TransformerNet`, `ConvTransformer`, and `ViTNet`. Screen the seven adjustable models and allow `JastrowNet` as an optional fixed-capacity small-ring reference. Retain legacy candidates by name. Also screen a named circular stride-one convolution with invariant site reduction and, if pursued, a named attention variant using periodic relative positions and invariant reduction. Any architecture or projection change must qualify independently. Verify claimed cyclic symmetry by applying translations to configurations. Circular padding precedes a flattened head in legacy `Conv1DNet`, stride-two pooling can alias translations, and ViT uses absolute positions. Disable dropout throughout for a deterministic wavefunction. Tabulate allocated and active trainable parameters, unused entries, fixed-sector gauge redundancies, and the cost of any explicit orbit projection. Apply the parameter cap to all allocated trainable scalars. [Model definitions](../../pythonTest/NeuralNetworkClass.py) · [Pooled convolution experiment](../../pythonTest/SystemSizeInvTest.py) · [Attention experiments](../../pythonTest/OtherNNtests.py)

Use $L=6$ to check Hamiltonian conventions, sector enumeration, shifts, residuals, normalization, and observable estimators against sparse ED. Use the positive ground-state vector in the occupation basis and minimize $1-F$, with $F=|\langle\psi_{\rm ED}|\psi_\theta\rangle|^2$ for normalized states. Compute normalization and overlap stably over all configurations in the fixed sector. All candidates use the same full-sector data and reduction at $L=8$ and, for the shortlist, $L=10$, including symmetry variants. Use $L=12$ only as an optional follow-up for qualifiers. Existing ED trainers provide model wiring and comparison routines, and the screen requires the direct squared-fidelity objective. [Sparse reference ED](../../CriticalBehavior/src/ed.py) · [Sector ED trainer](../../pythonTest/TrainEDstates.py) · [Full-basis ED trainer](../../pythonTest/TrainEDstatesFull.py)

- **Matched budget.** Start with parameter bands nearest 2,000, 8,000, and 32,000, all subject to a common 32,000-parameter cap. Log actual counts and any unsupported band. Use full-sector Adam and the same three learning rates $3\times10^{-4},10^{-3},3\times10^{-3}$ on one tuning seed per size, band, and phase. Give each tuning trial at most one quarter of the production budget. Select one configuration and band per candidate and size across all three phases using the worst tuning infidelity. Confirm each phase on three independent seeds disjoint from tuning. Proposed per-seed production ceilings are $5\times10^{13}$ counted training FLOPs at $L=8$ and $2\times10^{14}$ at $L=10$, including forward, backward and updates, with a common 3,000-step ceiling. Score at common fractional budget checkpoints and at the last complete step. Stop at the first ceiling or at a scored $1-F\le10^{-4}$. Freeze the protocol after the $L=6$ sanity check and before screening.
- **All-three gate.** Advance to $L=10$ after the independent-seed median reaches $1-F\le10^{-3}$ in each $L=8$ phase. Qualify for larger VMC only when that rule holds in every phase at both sizes. Report $10^{-4}$ crossings and all individual seed outcomes. A failing candidate can receive one retest per size if every missed phase has median $1-F\le2\times10^{-3}$. Resume the same configurations and three seed runs in missed cells to at most twice both original ceilings. Diagnose symmetry, objective agreement, numerical issues, and budget-limited versus stalled learning from trajectories. A miss means failure under this protocol. Carry at most one eligible RBM or dense baseline, one translation-invariant convolution, and one attention finalist. Rank within each slot by worst qualification median, then compute to the default gate. Leave a slot vacant if none of its candidates qualify.

Prior results motivate the comparisons. Saito and Kato tested dense and convolutional Bose–Hubbard ansätze. HubbardNet Fig. 9 compares runtime with ED on $L=3$–8 rings. Dash et al. study exact RBM infidelity in a spin-1 BKT setting, and Sehayek et al. study RBM learnability in Ising tomography. [Saito and Kato](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.05468) · [HubbardNet](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.5.043084) · [Dash et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01565) · [Sehayek et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.07532)

On a prespecified subset spanning all phases at $L=8$ and the BKT case at $L=10$, compare sampled VMC with exact energy optimization from matched initializations. Alongside the fidelity-trained results, this identifies sensitivity to the objective, optimizer and sampler. Start with Adam. If testing SR, use centered log-derivative covariances normalized by batch size with a fixed regularization convention. The legacy SR centers energy only and omits sample normalization in the solve, so its effective damping changes with batch size. Aim for reference shifts below one tenth of each acceptance tolerance. Use the spread of converged checks as an empirical numerical margin and flag unresolved cells. Reuse archived outputs with matching Hamiltonian, sector, cutoff, geometry, observable definitions, and adequate convergence evidence. ## Cost, scaling and reproduction

At $L=16,32$, compare two capacities per finalist and batches of 512, 2,048, and 8,192 retained configurations per update under a matched total training-compute ceiling. Hold the sampler proposal rule and optimizer convention fixed and allow update counts to vary. Count FLOPs per amplitude, including symmetry operations, and log all attempted proposals, burn-in, retained configurations, connected-state local-energy calls, backpropagation, and optimizer or SR solve costs. Record actual amplitude counts with cache reuse, training GPU-seconds and peak memory on the same GPU class, and separate reference, tuning, and independent evaluation costs. Report acceptance, autocorrelation, and effective sample size (ESS) for energy, each required $G_1$, and block variance. Forward FLOPs are also a reproducible proxy for comparison with work on 2D Heisenberg NQS. [Rende et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02794)

Report `P_to_target` and `chi_to_target` as the smallest tested reproducibly passing parameter count and smallest passing achieved maximum bond dimension. Include threshold brackets when the grid supports them, per-seed compute and samples to first confirmed crossing, and right-censored runs at their ceilings. Plot errors versus capacity and compute so failures and plateaus remain visible. Compare power-law, saturation and parsimonious crossover descriptions of the size trends, propagating seed and reference uncertainty. Fit through $L=48$ and record predictions before opening $L=64$ threshold results. Check sensitivity to the smallest size and to censored crossings, and leave the trend unresolved if those checks or the holdout cannot distinguish the forms. Report $L/\xi$ where finite and the largest reliably reproduced signal-bearing $G_1$ distance versus budget using simultaneous bounds across the measured profile.
